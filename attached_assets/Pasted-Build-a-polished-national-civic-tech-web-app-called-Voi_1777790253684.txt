Build a polished national civic-tech web app called “VoiceMap National: Animal Protection Portal.”

Purpose:
A free public-interest portal that helps people across the United States document animal welfare concerns, identify patterns, generate responsible report packets, and route concerns to appropriate local, state, federal, nonprofit, and media channels.

Important:
This app must not claim abuse has legally occurred. It should use careful language:
- “reported animal welfare concern”
- “alleged incident”
- “submitted evidence”
- “requires investigation”
- “pattern of reported concerns”
- “public concern summary”
The app should support responsible documentation, not public shaming.

Design style:
Use a premium MacOS Tahoe / Apple Liquid Glass interface:
- frosted glass panels
- translucent app cards
- luminous animated background orbs
- glowing hover and click borders
- subtle moving gradients
- dark navy, cyan, aqua, white glass, violet glow
- national civic dashboard aesthetic
- clean infographic style
- rounded cards
- simple icons
- professional and credible

Tech:
React + Vite + Tailwind CSS.
Use localStorage for version 1.
No backend required yet.
No paid APIs.
Structure app so Supabase/database/photo upload/email routing can be added later.

Jurisdiction Helper:
Create an interactive questionnaire that explains where concerns may go:
- Immediate danger: emergency / local law enforcement
- Stray animal concern: local animal services
- Shelter/facility concern: city/county agency or state oversight
- Interstate or organized issue: may require federal/state/nonprofit escalation
- Wildlife concern: state wildlife agency
- Online content: platform reporting plus possible law enforcement tip
Use disclaimer: “This is informational and not legal advice.”
